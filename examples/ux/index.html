How to review

  1. Open each variant page. Scroll through the iframes side-by-side.
  2. For ones you want to test seriously, click the ↗ "fullscreen" link to load that variant on its own.
  3. Try every comparison under different skins (selector top-right) — variant winners often look different across warm-serif vs midnight-mono.
  4. Try in dark mode (◐ toggle). Aurora orbs only show in dark.
  5. Tell me your picks. I lock them into the next PR — Tier 2 components, density tokens in glacial.css, data-aesthetic axis, etc.
