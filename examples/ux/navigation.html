Navigation

Four nav patterns for the same dashboard. The choice depends on (1) how many sections you have, (2) whether discovery or speed matters more, and (3) whether you want the brand front-and-center.

2026 trend: full sidebar + Cmd+K is becoming the standard combo (Linear, Vercel, Raycast, Notion). Mini-rail is the persisted-collapsed default.

C1 · Top nav (current default) ↗
ProsAll sections visible · Brand prominent · Mobile-friendly
ConsCaps at ~6 items · Wraps on narrow screens · Wastes vertical space
C2 · Full sidebar ↗
ProsSection grouping · Item counts · Scales to many destinations
Cons240px less content · Nav competes with content · Tablet/mobile awkward
C3 · Mini rail ↗
ProsMax content area · Linear/Notion default · Touch-friendly
ConsIcon-only ambiguous · Tooltips don't work on mobile · Less discoverable
C4 · Cmd+K only ↗
ProsZero chrome · Fastest for power users · Search + nav unified
ConsHostile to first-timers · Discoverability problem · No mobile equivalent

My recommendation

For a personal-tools-builder: C3 (mini-rail) + Cmd+K both ship. Rail handles the 6-12 destinations you have on any project; Cmd+K handles the speed once you know it. Top nav stays as a fallback for marketing pages or apps with ≤4 destinations. Drop the keyboard-first-only mode (C4) — your apps need to be discoverable to the version of you that hasn't touched the project in 3 months.

If you adopt this: build .glacial-rail + .glacial-rail-item as Tier 2 components. Add a glacialOpenPalette() helper to glacial.js. Document the keyboard contract in DESIGN.md (Cmd+K opens, ↑↓ navigates, Enter activates, Esc closes).
