Aesthetic temperament

Three reactions to the AI-slop default. Polished is what glacial ships today: smooth blur, soft shadows, generous radii. Brutalist is the 2026 reaction: 1px hard borders, offset shadows, square corners, monospace headings, no glass. Hybrid keeps glacial's atmosphere but borrows brutalist edges — the best of both, possibly.

2026 trend (NN/g, Fireart, brutalism.plus): "tactile brutalism" pushes back against AI-generated polish. The point isn't ugly — it's a deliberate refusal of fake-perfection.

E1 · Polished (current default) ↗
ProsCalm · Pretty · Modern · Aurora atmosphere · Wide appeal
ConsRisk: looks AI-generated · Generic 2024-Apple-clone vibe · Can feel weightless
E2 · Neo-brutalist ↗
ProsDistinctive · Tactile · Reads as "human-made" · Memorable
ConsLoud · Tiring on long sessions · Loses skin variety · No glass = loses glacial DNA
E3 · Hybrid (glass + brutal edges) ↗
ProsKeeps aurora · Earns its polish via discipline · Monospace headings tie to glacial's typography spec · Distinctive without being loud
ConsMore opinionated · Hard offset shadows compete with skins that disable orbs (warm-serif, nord)

My recommendation

Keep E1 (polished) as the default. Ship E3 (hybrid) as an opt-in data-aesthetic="hybrid" mode for projects where you want to dial in distinctiveness — your homepage dashboard, control-plane, anything you'd rather not look "generic." Skip E2 brutalism wholesale: it loses glacial's identity.

If you adopt this: ship data-aesthetic="hybrid" as a third orthogonal axis (alongside data-theme and data-skin). Document it in DESIGN.md. Add window.glacialSetAesthetic(name).
