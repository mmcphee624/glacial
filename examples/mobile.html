A focused page for verifying mobile behaviors. Open this on your phone (via cloudflared tunnel or LAN IP) to test touch targets, breakpoint behavior, and scroll-snap pills with real fingers.

Touch target audit

Apple/Google guidelines: every interactive element should be at least 44 × 44px. Toggle the overlay to highlight every button, link, pill, status row, and form input. Anything visibly smaller than the dashed outline is a violation.

Glass blur policy

--blur auto-reduces from 20px → 12px at <768px, then to 8px on coarse-pointer devices at <540px. Five stacked glass cards on iOS Safari at full blur drops below 30 FPS during scroll — this policy keeps it smooth.
